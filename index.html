01 / Business question

How I approached the problem

Why I built MarketAtlas

How would you find out how many petrol stations are in a specific area?

A senior HR professional asked me this practical question. I turned it into an independent, AI-assisted Business Information Systems project: define geography, obtain evidence, validate it, analyse it and communicate the result.

From question to implementation

Guang Quan Tan · Independent BIS portfolio. The full implementation remains private. The public showcase contains original system captures, a synthetic interactive demo and technical documentation.

04

Spatial joins, distance buffers, coverage, density, ranks and finance.

05

Communicate

Dashboard, casebook, reviewed snapshot and decision brief.

From a count to a decision

The Johor research scope grew into ten industries, three distance scenarios and a road-based screening workflow. It separates market coverage from actual demand and supports further investigation rather than automatic investment recommendations.

What I wanted to demonstrate

Requirements, data acquisition and quality, SQL and data modelling, analysis, interface design, research governance, API integration and stakeholder communication, with AI-assisted implementation and iterative testing.

04 / Evidence and methodology

Where the data comes from

SourceUse in the local system
OpenStreetMap / GeofabrikOSM PBF snapshot 2026-11-24T21:21:23Z; selected exact sector tags; node/way/area-relation objects. Earlier fuel-specific analysis used a separate Overpass snapshot.
GeofabrikMalaysia–Singapore–Brunei extract; selected road classes for road samples and mapped facility extraction.
WorldPop2026 R2025A v1 alpha constrained ~100 m modelled population grid; positive pixel centres assigned to districts and projected for distance.
DOSM2026 district population reference; separate from WorldPop; not used to rescale the raster.
geoBoundariesMalaysia ADM2 administrative boundaries; boundary membership and clipping.
CARTO VoyagerDisplay-only basemap; local proxy sends tile requests; not the source of facility counts.
The counts represent mapped OSM objects or facility–industry memberships, not a verified census of operating businesses. Population is modelled, not observed demand. Coverage uses straight-line buffers, not travel time. Candidate points are road samples, not available land. The client briefs are hypothetical; the retail operating data are synthetic and the financial results are illustrative. No commercial deployment, client outcome or realised savings are claimed.
